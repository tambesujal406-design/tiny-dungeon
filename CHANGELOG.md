# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- 5 switchable colour looks: Classic, Neon, Blood, Amber, Ice
- Optional CRT scanline and vignette mode
- Look and CRT choices are saved on the device

## 1.0.0

- 10 rooms with slimes, chasing ghosts, keys, doors, and heart pickups
- 3 hearts that carry across rooms, game over returns to room 1
- Title screen, smooth movement, and particle effects
- Chiptune background music with a mute toggle
- Room checker that validates and solves every room
- GitHub Pages deploy and CI workflows
